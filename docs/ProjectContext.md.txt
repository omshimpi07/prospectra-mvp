# 1. PRODUCT

## Working concept

A **Prospect Intelligence Platform** for people/small teams selling services to local businesses.

The product helps a seller answer:

> "Given what I sell, what kind of businesses should I target, which businesses actually fit that target, what evidence suggests they may need my service, and what is the best available way to contact them?"

The product is **not primarily a cold-calling application**.

The important problem is the work that happens before outreach:

```text
Find businesses
→ Research them
→ Determine relevance
→ Identify useful signals
→ Find legitimate contact routes
→ Prioritize prospects
→ Let the salesperson decide who to contact
```

The platform automates the repetitive research and prioritization work while keeping the final sales decision with the human.

# 3. CORE USER PROBLEM

The salesperson's real bottleneck is not making the call.

It is:

> finding enough genuinely relevant prospects per hour of salesperson effort.

The north-star concept is therefore:

**Useful / qualified prospects per hour of salesperson effort**

Not:

* number of businesses scraped
* number of records collected
* number of AI-generated leads
* number of websites found

Quality and usefulness matter more than raw volume.

# 7. PRODUCT PRINCIPLES

The following principles are approved and should guide implementation.

### 1. Deterministic before AI

Use normal software for things that can be computed reliably.

### 2. AI for interpretation

Use AI where semantics or natural-language reasoning are actually required.

### 3. Evidence over AI confidence

Every important AI-derived claim should be tied to supporting evidence.

### 4. Unknown is valid

Missing information should not automatically mean "No".

Valid states include:

```text
MATCH
NO_MATCH
UNKNOWN
PARTIAL
```

### 5. Partial results are better than total failure

One failed provider call or website should not destroy an entire search.

### 6. Human approval before outreach

The MVP does not autonomously contact prospects.

### 7. Provider-agnostic design

External providers must be isolated behind interfaces/adapters.

### 8. Cheap before expensive

Do cheap filtering first and expensive AI processing later.

### 9. Prospect score is contextual

A business's score depends on the selected seller ICP.

### 10. Do not make unsupported claims

Never invent:

* owner information
* phone numbers
* email addresses
* revenue
* business size
* intent
* buying probability
* business problems

Only state what evidence supports.

# 14. BUSINESS VS PROSPECT

This distinction must remain intact.

A `Business` is the underlying business entity.

A `Prospect` is the evaluation of that business for a specific:

```text
workspace
+
ICP
```

Therefore:

```text
Business ≠ Prospect
```

The same business can be:

* highly relevant to one seller
* irrelevant to another seller
* uncertain for another ICP

Scoring and qualification belong to the prospect context, not as a universal truth about the business.

```text

```


Also enforce:

* request timeout
* response-size limits
* redirect validation
* safe DNS resolution
* rate limiting

External web content is untrusted input.

# 36. IDEMPOTENCY

A worker may crash after doing work but before marking the job as completed.

Therefore operations should be idempotent.

Example:

Running the same website analysis twice should update/reuse the corresponding analysis result instead of generating duplicate records.

Do not assume a job will execute exactly once.

# 45. FAILURE HANDLING

Failures should be isolated.

Bad:

```text
One failed website
 ↓
Entire search fails
```

Good:

```text
One failed website
 ↓
Candidate marked partial/unverified
 ↓
Other candidates continue
```

Retry temporary failures such as:

* timeout
* temporary provider error
* rate limit
* temporary network issue

Do not blindly retry permanent failures such as:

* invalid input
* invalid URL
* unsupported response
* blocked internal destination

Every failure should be observable.
